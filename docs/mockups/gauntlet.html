Gauntlet page

The wheel stays exactly as it is — covers in segments, pointer on the right, Spin in the middle — and only its place on the page moves. Around it, the layout adds three things the page lacks today: an explanation that changes with the mode, the applied filters as the same removable pills Games and Lists use, and a winner laid out as a composed card instead of a list of labels. Use the switches — or the mode cards themselves — to see both modes, before and after a spin.

Mode panel over wheel and winner

One full-width panel on top: the two modes as large cards to choose from on the left, and on the right what the chosen mode does and what is on the wheel right now. Below it the wheel sits on the left and the winner fills the right; before the first spin that column tells you what to do, so nothing on screen is empty.

  • The mode cards are one radio group and write the same setRoyal as the Settings drawer toggle.
  • The Royal card shows how many games are crowned; with none, it still switches and the wheel side explains how to crown one.
  • Below 900px the wheel stacks above the winner; the steps collapse to one column.

Filter pills are the shared AppliedFilters: removing one updates the same query that the Filters drawer writes, and “Clear all” keeps nothing but the mode. In Royal mode filters do not apply, so the same row lists the games on the wheel, with the ones already knocked out struck through.

The winner keeps every field it shows today — year, type, platforms, genres, modes, themes, languages, stats, summary, storyline, media and release dates — but the long ones move under a “More about the game” disclosure so the first view fits without scrolling.

Colours, spacing and radii are the project's own tokens, generated from apps/web/src/lib/app/styles/vars by bun --filter web sync:tokens. The wheel is redrawn from covers-like placeholders; game data is sample content. Hanken Grotesk stands in for ApercuPro.
