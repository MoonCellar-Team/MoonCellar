MoonCellar · game page, side column

Ratings, time to beat and achievements

Today the four sections each lay out their tiles differently: HowLongToBeat squeezes three labels into tiles that are too narrow, so "Completionist" touches the edge; VNDB's "6h 0m" wraps onto two lines; the achievement tiles are a different width again. Three ways to make them one block. Every option is shown at the desktop column width and at phone width, with a short game (Ever 17) and a long one (Persona 5 Royal) to stress the numbers.

Option A

Rows

Each section becomes a list of label and value rows inside one panel. Labels get the whole width, so nothing is cut or wrapped, and numbers line up on the right edge.

  • HowLongToBeat and VNDB stay separate sections, as on the page today.
  • Works at any width without a breakpoint; the most compact option.

Option B

Even tiles

Keeps the tiles, but every section uses the same three-column grid, so every tile has the same width and height. Values carry their unit ("5 h") on one line, and labels are shortened to fit one line: Main, Main + extra, 100%.

  • Closest to what is on the page now; only StatTile layout and the copy change.
  • VNDB shows whole hours ("6 h"; minutes only when under 10 hours: "4 h 30 m").

Option C

Time scale

"Time to beat" becomes one scale: Main story, Main + extra and Completionist stack as growing bars on a shared track, with the VNDB average as a marker on it. The numbers sit in a two-column legend below. Achievements turn into compact chips.

  • Shows at a glance how much longer 100% takes than the story.
  • Most visual, but the most new code: a new bar component.

Colours, spacing and radii are the project's own tokens, generated from apps/web/src/lib/app/styles/vars. Hanken Grotesk stands in for ApercuPro. Widths: 380 px for the desktop side column, 328 px for a phone.
