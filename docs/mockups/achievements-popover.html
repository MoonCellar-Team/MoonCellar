RetroAchievements Popover

The trophy on a game card opens a popover instead of a modal. On desktop it is the shared Popover, anchored under the trophy and aligned to its right edge; at 500px and below the same component becomes the bottom sheet. Each direction is shown four ways: desktop and mobile, with awards earned and with none. The sample game is Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 4, which has sets on four consoles.

Option A

Compact list

One row per set: icon, console, a line of detail, and the award as a coloured pill. The whole row opens the set on retroachievements.org.

  • Fits the standard 320px popover width with no changes to Popover.
  • Earned sets come first, newest award on top; the rest show the number of achievements in the set.
  • With no awards, every icon is greyed out and a one-line hint sits above the list.
  • On mobile the rows get a panel background and 48px icons, so each is an easy tap target.
Option B

Awards first

The viewer's awards are the content; sets without one shrink to chips under "Also on".

  • A summary line counts the sets with an award, with one segment per set.
  • Each award is a card with an icon ringed in the award colour, the date and the award type.
  • With no awards, the summary and cards are replaced by a hint, and the chips are titled "Available on".
  • The tallest of the three with awards; with many earned sets the sheet scrolls.
Option C

Icon tiles

A grid of set icons with the award as a corner badge, the way a RetroAchievements profile shows them.

  • Two columns in the desktop popover, three in the sheet.
  • The icon art does the work of telling sets apart; the date is dropped to keep tiles square.
  • With no awards, the tiles are greyed out and the legend is replaced by the hint.
  • A game with a single set leaves one lonely tile. Fall back to a row from Option A there.

Needs from the API, for every direction: each set's imageIcon and numAchievements. Both are stored in the RetroAchievements games collection but not sent with the game today. Icons here are placeholders, because a published mockup cannot load images from media.retroachievements.org. Colours, spacing and radii are the project's tokens, generated into the :root block by bun --filter web sync:tokens. Hanken Grotesk stands in for ApercuPro, whose licensed file cannot be served from a third-party host.
