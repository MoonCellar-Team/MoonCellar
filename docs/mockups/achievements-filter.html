MoonCellar · Filters panel

Achievements filter

Five choices: every game, Steam, RetroAchievements, both or either. In one segmented row with full names they do not fit the 460 px filters panel: the last tab wraps onto a line of its own and the row turns into a ragged block. Four layouts, each at the desktop panel width and at 360 px for a phone, with the toggles that follow it. Every option is clickable.

Option A

Short labels, one row

The same segmented control, but "RetroAchievements" becomes "RA" with the RA medal and Steam gets its logo, so all five fit on one line. The full meaning of the current choice is spelled out to the right of the heading.

  • Smallest change: only the labels and icons differ from today.
  • "RA" relies on the icon and the hint; at 360 px the row is tight.

Option B

Two rows

The sources on the first row (All, Steam, RetroAchievements), the combinations on the second (Both, Either), each row split evenly. Full labels, no orphan.

  • Reads at a glance and keeps full names.
  • Takes two rows of height.

Option C

One switch per source

Two small three-way switches, Steam and RetroAchievements, each Any / With / Without. "Both" is With + With and either source alone is With + Any. It also covers cases the tabs cannot, such as Steam but not RA.

  • Most expressive and easy to extend with another source.
  • "Either" (Steam or RA) has no setting; the API would take two flags instead of one value.

Option D

Dropdown

A single field like the other filters in the panel. Closed it shows the current choice; open, each option has an icon and a one-line explanation.

  • One row of height and room to explain every option.
  • Hides the choices behind a click.

Colours, spacing and radii are the project's own tokens, generated from apps/web/src/lib/app/styles/vars. The panel is ExpandMenu at 460 px; Hanken Grotesk stands in for ApercuPro.
