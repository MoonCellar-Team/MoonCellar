MoonCellar · mobile popover

Popovers as bottom sheets on mobile

On a phone every popover — Add to list, rating, external pages, Regenerate — opens as a sheet from the bottom edge. The bar at the top is the grab handle: drag the sheet down past a third of its height, or flick it, and it closes; release earlier and it springs back. Tapping the dimmed page closes it too. Four shapes of the same sheet, all showing the Add to list panel so they compare like for like. Each phone is live: press Add to list, then drag.

A · edge sheet

Edge sheet

Full width, glued to the bottom, rounded top corners. Height follows the content up to 85% of the screen, then the list scrolls. The most familiar shape on both platforms.

B · floating card

Floating card

Inset from every edge with all corners rounded and the blurred panel of Box. Reads as the desktop popover grown to phone width, so the two feel like one component.

C · snap sheet

Snap sheet

Opens at half height; drag the bar up to expand it to almost full screen, down to collapse and then close. Suits long content such as a big list collection or the Regenerate prompt with the keyboard open.

D · action sheet

Action sheet

Centred rows in a floating card with a separate Cancel button under it. Best for short choices; for forms (Regenerate, rating) it would fall back to variant B.

Behaviour shared by all four: drag threshold is one third of the sheet height or a flick faster than 0.5 px/ms; the page behind is frozen through the same useDisableScroll lock modals use; the sheet takes env(safe-area-inset-bottom) as extra bottom padding; with reduced motion it opens and closes without the slide. Desktop keeps the anchored Popover unchanged.

Colours, spacing and radii are the project's own tokens: the :root block above is generated from apps/web/src/lib/app/styles/vars by bun --filter web sync:tokens. The typeface is substituted — Hanken Grotesk stands in for ApercuPro, whose licensed file cannot be served from a third-party host.
