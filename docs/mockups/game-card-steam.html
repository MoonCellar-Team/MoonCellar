MoonCellar · GameCard

Steam achievements on the game card

The card already has a RetroAchievements medal in the bottom-right rail. It shows the viewer's progress by colour (yellow mastered, green beaten, grey none) and opens the achievements popover. What goes on the card for Steam is the viewer's own progress: the unlocked and total counts from user.steam.achievements, read from their linked Steam account. A game the viewer has no Steam progress in shows nothing new; the game's total stays in the achievements popover.

The constraint is width. At the 160 px minimum the bottom rails already hold four 30 px cells: rating and status on the left, the RA medal and the average rating on the right. One more cell there touches the left rail. Each option is shown at 160 and 200 px, in five states.

Chosen · option A, revised

Steam cell with its own popover, rating moved up

The average rating moves from the bottom-right corner to the top-right rail, left of the info button. That frees the bottom-right rail for achievements only: the RA medal and, once the viewer has unlocked anything, a Steam trophy cell, grey while in progress and yellow when every achievement is unlocked.

  • Fits at 160 px in every state: each bottom rail holds two 30 px cells.
  • The trophy opens a Steam popover of its own with the viewer's progress; the medal keeps opening the RetroAchievements popover.

Option A

A second cell next to the medal

A Steam trophy cell joins the bottom-right rail once the viewer has unlocked anything, coloured like the medal: yellow when they have every achievement, grey while in progress. Clicking it opens the same achievements popover with a Steam section.

  • Least new code and the clearest: one icon per source.
  • At 160 px with both sources, the bottom rails meet (dashed cells) and the title loses its last line.

Option B

One achievements cell

The medal cell becomes a general achievements cell. With one source it shows that source's icon; when the viewer has Steam progress and the game has an RA set, the two icons overlap in the same 30 px square. The colour is the viewer's best progress across both. The popover lists each source with its own progress.

  • Never wider than today, so it fits at 160 px in every state.
  • Needs the popover to explain which source is mastered.

Option C

Progress in the top-left rail

The top-left rail only holds the Royal crown, so the viewer's Steam progress goes there with its numbers: "12/49" while in progress, the trophy and "49" once mastered.

  • The only option that shows the numbers without a click.
  • Achievements are split between two corners: Steam top, RA bottom.

Option D

Progress bar on the cover edge

A 4 px bar along the bottom edge of the cover, between the rails, filled to the viewer's Steam progress and yellow when complete.

  • Takes no rail space and reads at a glance in a long grid.
  • The source is only clear from the tooltip; a bar next to an RA medal may read as RA progress.

Colours, spacing and radii are the project's own tokens, generated from apps/web/src/lib/app/styles/vars; the cells copy GameCard.module.scss (30 px, --color-bg-primary-tint, a 2 px --color-bg-secondary border). Covers are gradients because the mockup cannot load images. Hanken Grotesk stands in for ApercuPro.
